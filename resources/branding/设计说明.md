使用内置图像生成工具制作，后按用户要求为小猫添加“七七”名牌。PNG为最终主图，ICNS为macOS图标格式；16至1024像素图标由系统工具缩放打包，未另画替代图。

最终资源：`qiqi-icon.png`、`Qiqi.icns`。

## 初始提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished original macOS application icon for a cute Chinese photo organizing app named 七七整理台. Icon artwork only, NO text or letters anywhere. A lovable mint-green kitten made from a softly rounded upright instant-photo card, two tiny cat ears, black dot eyes, a tiny happy curved mouth, pink blush cheeks, little paws hugging a sunny yellow photo folder; a small coral-pink tape tab at one upper corner. Its chest contains a simple miniature landscape/photo symbol, subtly suggesting sorted photographs. Kawaii, gentle, capable little organizing buddy. Match a Memphis / neo-brutalist native desktop UI: warm off-white squircle tile, bold near-black even outlines, a small crisp offset near-black drop shadow, flat clean vector-like color regions, excellent spacing and restrained details. Palette warm milk #F8F7F3, mint #7ED9C3, rose #FF4E8E, sunny #FFCE3A, ink #171717. One coherent mascot silhouette, instantly readable at 32px and 64px; large friendly face, no busy texture, no glossy 3D, no gradient, no extra objects or floating confetti. Precisely centered square composition with the full off-white rounded-square app tile and its crisp offset shadow contained within an 84% square footprint, leaving transparent padding around the outer tile so it sits naturally in the macOS Dock. Transparent background OUTSIDE the cream icon tile. High-quality finished production app icon, no mockup, no device, no presentation sheet.
